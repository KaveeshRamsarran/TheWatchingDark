Level I — The Watchers move only when you are not looking at them. Keep them in sight. Darkness eats your sanity; lamps, matches and your flashlight hold it back.

Level II — The Angels are the opposite: they charge while you look at them. Use the edge of your vision, and never stare.

Level III — The Drowned are blind and hunt by sound. Crouch, stay out of the deep pools, and freeze when you hear clicking. Even a match strike is loud.

Level IV — The Unseen only exist on camera. While they are in the viewfinder they cannot move. Static means one is close; the camera battery is precious.

Level V — The Watching Dark. You are inside it. Few creatures remain — the rest climbed out long ago. Keep your light on, keep moving down, and don't look up... until it asks you to.
